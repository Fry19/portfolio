About

Hi, I’m Nick.

I’m a UX/UI and graphic designer based in Michigan, with a passion for creating products and services that make the internet more accessible for people around the world.

I studied Experience Architecture at Michigan State University, earned the Google UX Design Certificate, and have designed for a VR streaming platform, a healthcare company and a national nonprofit. I’m always learning from the people around me and working to master the craft of creating experiences that are distinctive, engaging and easy to use.
