The Product

The project centers around the development of a movie theatre seat reservation app designed to streamline the process of booking movie tickets. In addition to ticket reservations, the app allows users to pre-order food and beverages for their theater visit, enhancing the overall movie-going experience.

The Problem

Users face significant limitations with existing movie ticketing apps, including the inability to pre-book tickets before a movie's release and the absence of a bookmarking feature for future viewing. These gaps lead to frustration and a fragmented user experience.

The Goal

The primary objective was to design an intuitive and seamless app that allows users to easily browse nearby movie theaters, book tickets, reserve seats, pre-purchase food items in advance, and conveniently bookmark their favorite movies. The app aimed to provide a comprehensive solution that addresses the unmet needs of moviegoers.​

01

Understanding the User

User Research: Summary

User research was conducted in several phases, starting with the creation of personas to better understand user frustrations and desires when booking movie tickets. This research revealed key pain points and feature requests, which informed the design process.

The next phase involved conducting a usability test on the low-fidelity prototype. Feedback from this testing was critical in refining the app's user experience, ensuring it was both intuitive and accessible to a broad audience.

Pain Points

  • Advanced Booking: Users expressed frustration over the inability to book tickets before a movie's release. To address this, I ensured that the app included a feature to view and book tickets for upcoming movies.

  • Bookmark Feature: Users wanted the ability to bookmark movies for later viewing. I implemented a bookmarking feature that allows users to reserve tickets directly from their saved list.

  • Connection Issues & Bugs: Users were concerned about the reliability of existing apps. I prioritized creating a smooth, bug-free experience, with a focus on stability and performance.

  • Trailer Unavailability: Many apps lacked movie trailers, a feature users found valuable. I included the option to watch trailers before booking tickets.

02

Personas

Robert, an electrical engineer, needs a bug-free ticket reservation app to conveniently book tickets anywhere in the country.

Bianca, a school teacher, needs a reliable app that allows her to pre-book tickets and collect them without waiting in line.

Journey Map

User Goal: Find a reliable, bug-free app to browse and book tickets for favorite movies efficiently.

03

Design Process

Low-Fidelity Wireframes

After conducting brainstorming sessions and surveys, I created low-fi wireframes based on the data gathered from user research. The focus was on identifying the main tasks users wanted to accomplish and designing a user-friendly interface that facilitated these tasks.

High-Fidelity Wireframes

The low-fi wireframes were then translated into high-fi wireframes, offering a more refined visual representation of the app's structure and flow.

01 / 03

Low-Fidelity Prototype

A low-fidelity prototype was developed from the digital wireframes, and usability testing was conducted to gather further insights from users.

04

Usability Testing

Findings

To refine the app, I conducted two rounds of usability testing with five participants each. Key findings included:

  • Round 1:

    • Users requested more sign-in options.

    • The process of selecting a theater, date, and showtime needed simplification.

    • Scannable payment methods were desired.

  • Round 2:

    • The search bar needed to be more conveniently placed.

    • The logout button should be accessible within the menu.

    • The bookmark icon needed to be functional and easy to use.

Refined Screens

Based on the usability findings, I refined the app's screens, ensuring all key features were easy to access and use.

Final Mockup

After implementing changes based on usability testing, I created final mockups, which represented the polished version of the app

High-Fidelity Prototype

A high-fidelity prototype was developed to conduct the final round of usability testing with five participants.

Accessibility Considerations

  • Voice Assistive Technology: Integrated to accommodate users who are unable to type.

  • Language Options: Multiple language options were provided to cater to users who are not proficient in English.

  • Seat & Screen Type Variety: A range of seat and screen type options were included to ensure accessibility for a diverse user base.

05

Going Forward

Impact

After finalizing the design, I conducted an unmoderated usability test with eight participants. All participants were able to complete their tasks with ease, and two participants specifically mentioned that they enjoyed interacting with the interface and found it fun to use.

Given the increasing number of movie seat reservation apps entering the market, I believe this app has the potential to stand out due to its user-centered design and robust features.

What I Learned

This project highlighted the importance of user research in gathering essential data for design. The wireframing process proved invaluable in shaping the app's UI, while usability studies played a crucial role in refining the design. Attention to detail and a focus on solving user problems were key to delivering a successful app.
