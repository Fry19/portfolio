01

The Problem

This wasn’t only a navigation problem. With dealers and the general public sharing the same pages, the site was confusing for both.

Problem Statement

Visitors to the Counter Solutions by Lumbermen’s website often struggle to navigate it, and non-dealers frequently run into information meant for dealers. Because there’s no dedicated space for dealers, the site is confusing, and both dealers and general visitors have a hard time finding what they need.

02

Overview

I set out to make the site easier to navigate and give dealer information its own place behind a dealer login, handling everything from client interviews to visual design myself.

Project Goal

My goal was to redesign the website for smoother navigation and to separate dealer information from public content. I planned a dealer login using a dealer ID, streamlined product information, a new FAQ section, and an articles page, along with a refreshed visual theme that better represents the brand and its products.

Roles & Responsibilities

UX Designer, UI Designer, UX Strategist, and Visual Designer.

User research, user strategy, client interviews, wireframing, prototyping, and visual design.

03

Research

Dealers and homeowners don’t need the same website. The research identified an audience running from kitchen and bath dealers to homeowners, and pointed to separate portals tailored to each role.

Product Users

To understand user needs, I interviewed the client, reviewed the Statement of Work, and created an information architecture map. The research showed that the main audience includes kitchen and bath dealers, builders, contractors, interior designers, and homeowners. My focus for the redesign was to clearly differentiate the dealer and homeowner experiences, so both feel welcome and can reach the right information.

User Needs

Users need a more personalized, streamlined experience across the site. Dealers and homeowners need separate portals, each with content and functionality tailored to their role.

User Challenges

  • A website that’s hard to navigate
  • Dealer-only information mixed into public pages
  • No dedicated space for dealers
  • Dealers and general visitors alike struggling to find what they need
